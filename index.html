Biography

I am currently a PhD student in the Artificial Intelligence Thrust at the Information Hub of The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou). Previously, I received the B.Eng. degree in Electronic Information Engineering from Southwest University in 2023 and the M.Phil. degree in the Robotics and Autonomous Systems Thrust from HKUST(GZ) in 2025. I was a visiting scholar at the School of Vehicle and Mobility, Tsinghua University in 2025.

My research interests include control theory, optimization, multi-agent systems, reinforcement learning, and their applications in intelligent transportation systems.
